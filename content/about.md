Software engineer from Jakarta, Indonesia. 5+ years building Android and mobile apps for banking, telecom, and enterprise clients.

I care about the work underneath the surface — architecture that holds up at scale, codebases that new people can actually understand, and the kind of technical debt that's worth paying down before it compounds.

## what i do

**android** — Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, Ktor, Koin, Room, Coroutines. Currently at SALT, maintaining and building features for BCA Life's mobile insurance platform.

**mobile** — Flutter for cross-platform work when it fits. Riverpod, Melos, Mason Bricks.

**systems** — build tooling, Gradle convention plugins, CI/CD pipelines. I've migrated projects off scattered root-level `.gradle` files into proper `build-logic/` setups and care more about this than most people think is reasonable.

**learning** — currently going deep on Kotlin Multiplatform. The promise of sharing business logic without compromising native UI is worth the rough edges.
