I work at the intersection of structured systems and expressive form.

Trained in graphic design, self-taught in software. I believe the best interfaces are built from the same principles as the best posters: **clarity**, **hierarchy**, and **intention**.

The Bauhaus taught that form and function are not opposites — they are the same question asked twice. I try to ask it once, carefully.

## currently

Building design tooling and writing about type. Available for select projects — particularly those where the visual language matters as much as the functionality.

## background

Studied at Central Saint Martins. Worked at studios in London and Berlin before going independent in 2022. Since then: smaller clients, better work.
